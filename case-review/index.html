Start your case review

We’ll start with the original delivery date, then collect the facts needed to evaluate a Florida Lemon Law claim.

Florida’s Lemon Law rights period generally ends 24 months after original delivery (Fla. Stat. § 681.102(9)). If you’re outside that window, we stop there. If you’re in it, we ask for the full document packet next. Fee terms and an engagement agreement for electronic signature come only after that packet is in and you reply “I want to proceed.”
